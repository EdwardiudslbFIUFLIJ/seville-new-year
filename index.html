Price by group size

The price per person barely moves. Flights cost the same each for anything up to 10, and the accommodation grows by adding or dropping a flat. Odd numbers like 5, 7 or 9 cost a little more each because someone's paying for an empty bed.

Where to stay

All entire flats or houses on Airbnb, free over New Year when checked. They come in 4s, 6s and 10s, so we can combine them to fit however many come. Splitting across smaller flats is usually cheaper than one big house.

AlfalfaBest for being in it

Tapas and wine bars, busy at night, between the main squares and Santa Cruz. Plaza Nueva is next door.

AlamedaBest for nightlife

The bar strip, bohemian and lively. A short walk north of the centre.

Santa CruzBest for sights

Walk to the Cathedral and Alcázar, plenty of restaurants. Arenal is just beside it.

TrianaAcross the river

Riverside bars and flamenco, a little further to walk into the centre.

MacarenaCheaper, further out

Outside the main centre. Fine for sleeping, longer walks back.

Small print

Before anyone pays

Flights

  • Fares rise as seats sell. Saturday 2 Jan had only 1 seat left at the cheapest price, so book everyone together once people commit.
  • Add a 10 kg cabin bag each way if you want one.
  • Fares were identical for 6 and 10 seats. Booking 16 raised them by £12–£20 each.
  • Coming out Tuesday 29th instead costs £126.50 more each, plus an extra night.
  • Flexible on the way back? Monday 4 Jan was £100.99, about half the weekend price.

Accommodation

  • Places go fast over New Year. Everything here was free on 18 Oct.
  • Several flats have fewer beds than guests, so some people share. Bed counts are on each card.
  • EDomus Muralla wasn't bookable for 30 Dec – 2 Jan, only to the 3rd.
  • Most show free cancellation, but read each listing's terms and house rules. Parties are usually banned.
